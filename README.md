# Restaurant Management System

A proposed system connecting customer QR ordering, waiter POS, kitchen preparation, table service, and restaurant management in one workflow.

- **Ordering and service:** send validated orders directly to the kitchen, track progress, and notify the assigned waiter.
- **Menu and stock:** calculate dish availability from recipes and usable inventory; reserve ingredients when orders are accepted and consume them when preparation starts.
- **Management:** manage tables, waiter zones, suppliers, stock movements, and recipes, with reporting on sales, kitchen performance, and waste.
- **Settlement:** cashiers confirm payments handled outside the system.

The concept starts with one restaurant and requires an online connection. Prepared-batch tracking is optional. Technology selection and implementation planning remain open.
